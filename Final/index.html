Introduction

For a long time, the standard advice has been to just grind out more hours. But true learning isn’t about self-punishment; it’s about strategy and environment. When you study completely on your own, it’s tough to stay accountable. Without the right structure, a five-minute break easily transforms into a two-hour social media spiral. And without a supportive community, hitting a difficult concept can feel like running into a brick wall. We believe that you shouldn't have to figure out how to learn all by yourself.
